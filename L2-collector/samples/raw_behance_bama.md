"Bama Tea" gift box packaging design
  

The tea packaging is mainly red, with Chinese style elements such as carp and copper coins, full of joy. Each gift box contains 6 tea cans, symbolizing good luck. Tea is the best gift for Chinese people, and this kind of packaging is very popular.  
  
We are a custom packaging supplier,  
Providing packaging design and production services.  
  
Contact us for packaging solutions.
