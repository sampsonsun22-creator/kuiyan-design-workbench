天仁茗茶推出的綠茶蕎麥黃金捲禮盒，使用鐵盒為禮盒形式，加上紙張封套設計

以產品內容食材：綠茶、蕎麥、肉鬆等元素轉換成抽象的幾何圖形，與花草圖形交織成對稱花紋

並選擇以綠色、米色帶出食材意象。

This is the Green Tea Buckwheat Golden Roll Gift Box introduced by Ten Ren Tea in 2022.

The gift box is made of iron, accompanied by a paper sleeve design. The ingredients of the product, such as green tea, buckwheat, and pork floss, are transformed into abstract geometric shapes, interwoven with floral patterns to create a symmetrical design.

The colors chosen, green and beige, highlight the imagery of the ingredients.
