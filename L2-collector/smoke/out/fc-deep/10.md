> **The Brief**
>
> Taiwan tea packaging for tourists
>
> **Main Idea**
>
> Tell stories with the packaging: Cycling around Sun Moon Lake in spring days, Catch the mountain sunrise with hot tea and local friends.
>
> **The Design**
>
> The nicely packaged tea that uses infographic and illustrations to convey the product’s benefits, where the product was produced, helps the tourists to make the purchase decision. It’s not just a tea box, but also a special gift from Taiwan.
