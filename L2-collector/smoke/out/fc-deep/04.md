Theory of tea is a premium tea brand made for those who enjoy tea as an experience.

The font is inspired by the tea hills, sinuous, round, full and intense.

The tea is packaged in sustainable and recyclable cardboard boxes and the bag to contain the tea filter

is also made of paper. The illustrations are personalized, each floral design is hand drawn to give more

personality to each blend.
